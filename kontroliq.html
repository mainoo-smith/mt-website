Frequently Asked Questions

Answers teams ask before committing to a SOC 2 and ISO 27001 automation platform.

Do we keep control of our compliance data?

Yes. KontrolIQ is designed to run in your AWS boundary so sensitive evidence and telemetry remain under your governance controls.

How long does deployment take?

Teams can deploy quickly with AWS-native setup patterns, then tune scope and controls based on their SOC 2 readiness objectives.

Does it only detect issues, or support remediation?

It supports remediation workflows with actionable guidance, owner accountability, and status visibility through closure.

Is this only for startups?

No. KontrolIQ is built for security-conscious SaaS teams and regulated organizations that need continuous SOC 2 readiness and evidence discipline.
