Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common procurement and risk questions.

Can we deploy in our own environment?

Yes. KontrolIQ supports customer-controlled deployment models so you can run inside your own cloud or infrastructure boundary.

The architecture is designed to avoid unnecessary cross-border exposure by keeping sensitive compliance data and evidence processing local.

Do you only show findings, or support remediation?

KontrolIQ is built for action. It provides remediation guidance, owners, and status visibility so teams can close gaps quickly.

Is this aligned to Ghanaian regulatory context?

Yes. The product direction is informed by local audit practitioners and mapped to Ghana-focused control and reporting realities.
