What KontrolIQ Replaces and What It Is Not

KontrolIQ is the compliance solution built on Mainoo’s coordination core—focused on continuous, local-first compliance operations.

What KontrolIQ Replaces

  • Spreadsheet-heavy evidence tracking across teams
  • Manual audit preparation and last-minute file chasing
  • Point-in-time compliance snapshots without continuity
  • Fragmented remediation follow-up over email and chat

What KontrolIQ Is Not

  • Not a generic compliance dashboard with static checklists
  • Not a forced multi-tenant SaaS data export model
  • Not a consultancy-only engagement without product workflow
  • Not a once-per-audit toolkit disconnected from operations
