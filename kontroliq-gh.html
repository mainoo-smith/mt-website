Why KontrolIQ

Audit preparation is often manual, fragmented, and reactive. Evidence lives in emails, shared folders, and spreadsheets, and control documentation is frequently completed late in the audit cycle.

KontrolIQ was built to support how audits actually work in practice — by helping organizations maintain structured evidence and control visibility throughout the year.

KontrolIQ is not a generic GRC platform. It is designed around real audit workflows.

Structured Evidence

Systems and operational signals are monitored and structured as evidence over time — not just point-in-time documentation.

Audit-Ready Organization

Evidence is categorized and aligned to audit requirements, reducing manual preparation and last-minute scrambling.

Early Gap Resolution

Teams address gaps early instead of reacting during audit periods, reducing audit disruption and stress.
